# YouTube Doubt Solver

An AI assistant that helps you understand YouTube video content by answering your specific questions and clarifying doubts. Simply share a YouTube video URL, and get instant, contextual explanations without leaving the platform.

## Features

- **Video-Specific Answers**: Get responses that focus exclusively on the content of the video you share
- **Contextual Understanding**: Receive explanations that consider the full context of the video material
- **Concept Clarification**: Have difficult concepts from tutorials, lectures, or presentations explained in simple terms
- **Time-Stamped Responses**: Get references to specific moments in the video where information appears
- **Stay On Topic**: All explanations remain within the scope of the video content

## How It Works

1. Share a YouTube video URL
2. Ask your question about the video content
3. Receive a clear, concise explanation based solely on what's covered in the video

## Perfect For

- Students watching educational content
- Professionals following technical tutorials
- Anyone who needs clarification on video materials
